Content Strategy Thoughts — April 2026
Prioritize UGC & "iPhone Footage"
APRIL 2026
Reels are the top performer and engagement is climbing. Lean into raw, authentic content — gym floor clips, member transformations shot on phone, behind-the-scenes of classes. Polished content stops the scroll less than real moments do. Ask members to tag @bootybabeztampa and repost their stories/clips.
Hook-First Reels
Static posts with no hook underperformed — apply the same lesson to Reels. Lead with a bold text overlay or provocative question: "Nobody talks about this part of glute training" or "POV: your first BBZ class." The algorithm rewards watch-through rate, so the hook is everything.
Carousel Education Posts
Instead of dropping static posts entirely, convert them to carousel slides with tips, myths vs. facts, or "3 moves you're doing wrong." Carousels get saved and shared, which boosts reach without needing video.
Leverage Community & Location
BBZ is strong in Tampa with a loyal female audience. Create content that ties into the local scene — "Best post-workout acai in Channelside," collabs with nearby Tampa businesses, or a "Tampa girls who lift" series. This builds community and hits local search terms already ranking on Google.

Notes & Recommendations — July 2026
What Worked
TOP FORMAT: CAROUSELS
Member spotlights create social proof without feeling like an ad. Relatable, identity-based captions drove the highest engagement. Carousels dominated across the board. High-share content (like the free first class post with 19 shares) reaches beyond existing followers — people send it to friends who might benefit.
What Didn't Work
Single images consistently underperformed. Event/promotional posts fell flat — content with a lot of logistical detail reads as an announcement rather than a conversation, and the audience scrolls past it. Motivational quotes without a hook — captions aren’t consistently ending with a question or prompt that invites a response.
Recommendations for August
1. Add a CTA or question to every caption — no post should end without inviting a response.
2. More member spotlights — these are the top engagement drivers month over month.
3. Lean harder into carousels — they dominated top performing content this month.
4. Repurpose high-share content — the free class post got 19 shares; create variations of clear-value-prop content.
5. Avoid single-image event announcements — turn them into carousels with a hook on slide 1.
6. +207 new followers is strong growth — keep feeding the funnel with shareable, community-first content.
